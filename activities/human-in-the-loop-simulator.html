Your Mission

You'll face real-world scenarios where AI is offering to help. Your job is to decide how to respond in a way that keeps you in control of important decisions.

  • Review AI outputs before acting on them
  • Maintain oversight of consequential decisions
  • Use AI as a tool, not a replacement for judgment
  • Know when to override or question AI suggestions
