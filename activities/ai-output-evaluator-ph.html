AI Output Evaluator

AI replies sound finished whether they are right or wrong. Practice finding what needs a second look before you hit send.

United States Philippines

You will miss some. That is the point.

In each round you will read a detailed request and the reply an AI gave. The reply is well written and mostly right. Each reply is split into underlined segments, and we will tell you how many of them need a second look. Your job is to find which ones.

Expect to struggle: These replies are written the way real AI mistakes happen: quietly, inside good sentences. Missing things here is expected, and it is how the skill grows. There is no passing score, and nothing you do is saved.

Four questions help. Keep them in mind as you read:

  • Is it true?Did it add, change, or round off something I gave it? Is there a claim with no source?
  • Is it fair?Does it assume things about people that I did not say?
  • Does it fit what I asked?Did it quietly drop one of my details or limits?
  • Is it too sure?Did a maybe become a promise?
