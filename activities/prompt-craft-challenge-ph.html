Prompt Craft Challenge

AI answers the prompt you typed, not the situation in your head. Practice closing the gap between the two.

United States Philippines

AI cannot read your mind.

When a reply disappoints, the cause is often something you knew and never typed. In two rounds you will see a real situation, the quick prompt someone wrote, and the reply it produced. Your job is to find what never reached the AI, then rewrite the prompt yourself. In round 3 you will write a prompt for something you need this week.

Expect to struggle: The gaps are hard to see because the first prompt looks fine. Missing some is expected, and it is how the skill grows. There is no passing score, and nothing you write is saved or sent anywhere.

Four questions help. Keep them in mind as you read:

  • Who is it for?The reader or audience, and what I know about them.
  • What is it for?Where it will be used, and what should happen because of it.
  • What do I already know?The facts, names, and numbers the AI would otherwise guess.
  • What are the limits?Length, tone, what to avoid, and what I have to work with.
Three rounds, about 20 minutes.

    Keep private things private: Before you paste a prompt into an AI tool, take out anything you would not want stored, such as full names, account numbers, or details about students or clients.
