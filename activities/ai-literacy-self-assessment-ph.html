AI Literacy Self-Assessment

Find out where you stand, where you are stronger than you think, and where to start learning.

United States Philippines

A mirror, not a test.

You will read 17 short situations, one for each AI literacy competency named by researchers Duri Long and Brian Magerko. For each one, choose the strongest answer, then say how sure you are.

Expect to miss some: The answers are written to be close, because real understanding shows up in the close calls. Nobody is expected to get all 17. There is no passing score, and nothing you choose is saved.

Why we ask how sure you are: the most useful thing to learn is not your score. It is where you were certain and wrong, and where you were guessing and right.
