Learn by Doing

Interactive Activities

Hands-on experiences that teach human-centered AI through real scenarios and real decisions. Each one has a United States version and a Philippines version.

Activity 1

HCAI vs System-Centered AI

Explore the spectrum of AI approaches

Learn to identify and evaluate different AI approaches using Shneiderman's framework. See how real-world AI tools balance human control and automation, then apply your understanding to case studies.

  • 20 to 30 minutes
  • Case studies and a short check
United States version Philippines version
Activity 2

Human-in-the-Loop Simulator

Practice AI decision scenarios

Step into realistic scenarios where AI makes suggestions and you decide what to do. Practice maintaining human oversight across workplace, education, healthcare, finance, and hiring contexts.

  • 15 to 20 minutes
  • Decision simulation with a score
United States version Philippines version
Activity 3

AI Output Evaluator

Read AI answers with a critical eye

AI replies sound finished whether they are right or wrong. Read three realistic replies to detailed requests and mark what you would confirm before using them. The problems are subtle, and missing some is part of the learning.

  • 15 to 20 minutes
  • Three rounds, expect to miss some
United States version Philippines version
