    Look at the replies again. If you would choose a different tool than your scores did, ask which of the five questions mattered most for this job. That question is your real test.

    Before you settle on a tool

    • Cost: what does the free version limit, and would you hit that limit?
    • Privacy: check the settings for whether your chats are stored or used for training.
    • Access: does it work well on your device and your connection?
    • Language: does it handle the languages you and your readers use?
    • Test again: a new kind of task deserves a new test, and so does a few months passing.
