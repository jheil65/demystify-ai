A researcher that shows its sources. 📓

Most AI chatbots answer from everything they have absorbed, and when they do not know, they can fill the gap with a confident guess. Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) works differently. It answers from the documents, links, and videos you choose, and it adds numbered badges so you can check where each claim came from.

Part 1 · See the difference

📓 Gemini NotebookAnswers from your sources

Practice version, not live AI. The answers and the sample documents are made up to show the pattern.

Both answers sound confident. Only one can be checked. The general answer may be perfectly reasonable, but it is about the world in general. The notebook answer is about your documents, and it shows you the passage so you can judge it yourself.
Grounded does not mean perfect. In one 2025 study of questions about documents, a notebook tool still made a mistake in 13% of its answers, compared with 40% for general chatbots. Most mistakes were overconfident summaries, not invented facts. That is why the badges matter: tap them.

Part 3 · Choosing your sources

What can you put inside?

You do not need special file types. You can mix up to 50 sources in one notebook on the free plan. The notebook is only as good as what you put in, so choosing sources is the most important decision you make.

📄 PDFsManuals, circulars, handbooks, reports. If a scanned PDF will not load, the text may be a picture. Try a text version.
📘 Word, PowerPoint, ExcelWord files upload directly. If a slide deck or spreadsheet will not load, save it as a PDF first, or paste the key table as copied text.
📗 Google Docs and SlidesAdd them straight from your Google Drive.
🌐 Website linksPaste the address of a public page and the notebook reads its text. Some sites block this. If a link fails, copy the text in instead.
▶️ YouTube videosPaste the link to a public video that has captions. The notebook reads the transcript.
🎙️ Audio and copied textUpload a recording and it is turned into text. You can also paste in notes or an email thread.

🔑 Account tip: personal or work account? School and company Google accounts often block sharing a notebook outside the organization. If you need a shareable link for a course, a personal Gmail account is the smoother choice. Or use your work account and submit screenshots.
🛡️ Before you upload anything. Leave out personal data: student names with grades, customer phone numbers, ID numbers, health or pay records. Follow your organization's rules and your country's privacy law (in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act, RA 10173), and respect who owns a source (the IP Code, RA 8293).
Part 4 · The one move that changes your results

Customize before you generate

Imagine you uploaded a 60-page manual. The Studio can turn it into an Audio Overview, a kind of podcast with two AI hosts. If you tap Generate straight away, it tries to cover all 60 pages. Compare what happens when you choose Customize first and say what you need.

📋 Three instructions to copy and adapt

Tap a card to copy it. Replace the parts in [brackets] with your own details.

Your job as the human: you are the editor. Listen to or read what the Studio made before you share it. It can stress the wrong point, mispronounce local names, or sound more certain than your source is.

What to take with you

You choose the sources. You check the claims.

📚
The notebook only knows what you give it.Good sources in, useful answers out. An outdated policy or a weak web page gives you confident answers about the wrong thing.
🔢
Tap the citations that matter.A numbered badge is an invitation to check, not proof. If an answer has no badge, treat it as a guess.
🛡️
Keep people's private details out.Remove names and personal data before you upload, even in a notebook only you can see.
