    What to take with you
    
Names change. Your thinking is portable.

    

      
🏷️
The feature got a new name, not a new idea.A Gem, a custom GPT, an agent, and a skill are all saved directions for the AI. If you could write one, you can write the others.

      
📄
Keep your directions in your own notes.Tools retire features, sometimes with only a few weeks of warning. Directions saved in a document you own can move to any tool, including free ones.

      
🧑‍🤝‍🧑
A person still checks the work.Fewer copy and pastes is convenient. It does not make the answer correct. The "stop and ask me" line is yours to write.

    

    
💬 Talk about it with your group
Who gets left behind when a tool retires a feature with a deadline? Think about a colleague with a free account, a school account, or a slow connection. What is one thing your team could do so nobody loses their work?
