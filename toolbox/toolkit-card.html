Before you pack · a two-minute tour

First time here? Welcome.

Demystifying AI for All is a free site made to go with your GLA courses. You may have seen the link in SPARK or Foundations and never opened it, and that is completely fine. There is no login, and you can keep using it long after ENGAGE ends.

The big idea: there is more under the hood. In the first two courses, most of us used AI as a chat box: type a question, read the answer. The same tools can also read your documents, make pictures, build small apps, research a question with sources, tutor you, and slow down to think. We call these powers. Each AI tool gives them a different name, but the jobs are the same. ENGAGE asks you to create with AI, so this activity makes sure you have tried the powers first.

What is on the site

The AI Toolbox ↗Try six powers hands-on, then compare five tools: Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, and DeepSeek. You will spend the most time here. Skill Builder ↗Write your directions once so AI does a task your way every time. Prompt library and Portable AI ↗The Learn AI page. Ready-to-copy prompts sorted by purpose, plus Portable AI templates that work in any free account. AI Playground ↗Short games that show how AI works on the inside. Keep learning ↗On the Learn AI page: AI Literacy, a Glossary, Podcasts and YouTube, and Human-Centered AI.

You do not need to visit all of these now. The five souvenirs below walk you through them one at a time, starting with the Toolbox.
