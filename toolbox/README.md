## Ground rules (same as the Playground)

- No logins, no grades, nothing a learner types is saved or sent anywhere.
- Every activity is a practice version, not live AI, and says so.
- Every power ends with "Your job as the human" and a free-account tip.

## What goes out of date, and where to fix it

Product names, dates, and free limits change. All of them live near the top of each file's `<script>` section. Change only the words inside the quotation marks.

- **Toolbox (`index.html`):** under `POWERS`, each power has a `names` list in this order: Gemini, ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, DeepSeek. Each line is the name to look for, then a badge letter: `f` free with limits, `p` usually paid, `c` check your plan, `n` not offered. The tool descriptions are under `TOOLS`. The matching game is under `MATCH`.
- **Then and Now (`then-and-now.html`):** names and dates are under `TOOLS`. The chart is under `TABLE`.
- **Pack Your Toolkit (`toolkit-card.html`):** the questions are in the page text itself. Answers are kept only in the learner's own browser so they can leave and come back. Nothing is sent anywhere. The four Portable AI template names must match the homepage.
- **Skill Builder (`skill-builder.html`):** the starter examples are under `EXAMPLES`.
- After any update, change the "Last checked" date near the bottom of the page.
