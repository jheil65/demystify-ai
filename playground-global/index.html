How to use the Playground

  • On your ownPlay anytime, on your phone or computer. Mistakes are part of the fun.
  • With your groupEach game ends with one question to talk about. Share your answers with classmates or colleagues.
  • For trainersUse a game as a warm-up before a lesson. It works well on a projector, too.
